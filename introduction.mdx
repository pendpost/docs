## What pendpost is

pendpost is agent-operated social ops with a human approval gate. It is an operations layer for the agent-plus-human workflow, not another social scheduler. An agent (or you) drafts posts; a human approves before anything goes live. pendpost handles the planning, the scheduling, the brand checks, and the publishing engines, while keeping a person in the loop on every post that reaches a real account.

It is built for developers, agencies, and technical solopreneurs who want their tooling to be local, inspectable, and scriptable rather than a hosted black box.

## Positioning

pendpost is the operations layer between an agent that writes and a human who decides. The agent can plan a whole campaign, but by default you approve before anything ships. Publishing is gated, rate-limited, and protected by circuit breakers so an automated workflow stays inside the bounds a person set.

A note on responsible use: by default pendpost holds every post for your approval, and nothing publishes until you approve it. You can opt into auto-approve, an owner-only setting you can revoke, to let matching posts publish on their own and scale to fully hands-off. You own your own accounts and are responsible for each platform's terms of service.

## Local-first by design

- Zero runtime dependencies in the server.
- Plans are local JSON you can read and edit.
- Media is local.
- Secrets live in your own `.env`.
- The server binds `127.0.0.1` (loopback) by default.

There is no telemetry. Nothing phones home; there is nothing to opt out of because none exists. If telemetry is ever added it will be opt-in and documented.

## The five differentiators

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="1. Human approval gate" icon="shield-check">
    Every post carries an approval state (`draft`, `pending`, `approved`, or `rejected`). Both `draft` and `pending` render as the amber needs-approval status. The gate is fail-closed: a post with no approval will not publish. `plan_create_post` always creates a draft. Only `approve_post` and `reject_post` flip approval. Nothing publishes until it's approved. By default that's you; auto-approve is owner-only and revocable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Anti-ban circuit breakers" icon="circuit-board">
    Meta error 368 (an action block) trips a breaker that halts the Meta lane and never auto-resumes, because 368 carries no machine-readable clear time. A cadence cap defers (never drops) bursts, and a lane pause kill switch lets you stop publishing on demand.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Humanizer brand-lint" icon="spell-check">
    Captions are checked before publish against rules in an editable `rules.json`. The humanizer layer flags English AI-writing tells. Errors block; warns are advisory.
  </Card>
  <Card title="4. Honest native scheduling" icon="calendar-clock">
    Where a platform supports it (YouTube `publishAt`, Mastodon, WordPress, Ghost), pendpost uses native scheduling, so those posts go out even when your computer is off. Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X go out at their time from the optional 24/7 cloud service or from your computer. pendpost is honest about which cover and thumbnail mechanics actually apply per platform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="5. Dual interface" icon="layout-panel-left">
    A web dashboard and any MCP client drive the same contract. A parity test enforces that every capability ships on both faces.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
